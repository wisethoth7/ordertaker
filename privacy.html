Privacy Policy

Effective: June 15, 2026

1. What We Collect

From restaurant operators: name, email, phone, menu data, payment info (via Stripe). From callers: name, phone, order details, call recordings.

2. How We Use It

To operate the AI phone agent, process orders, send SMS confirmations, process payments, and improve our service.

3. Third-Party Services

Telnyx (telephony), Vapi (AI voice), Supabase (database), Stripe (payments), Resend (email).

4. Data Retention

Call recordings: 30 days. Order data: 12 months. Account data: 90 days after cancellation.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. Contact our support team.

6. Security

All data transmitted over HTTPS. Database encrypted at rest. API keys stored as environment secrets.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: Contact Support

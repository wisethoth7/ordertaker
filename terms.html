OrderTaker provides an AI-powered telephone answering service for restaurants operated by Nile Dreams Digital.

2. Pricing & Billing

OrderTaker is available in four subscription plans, billed monthly per location:

  • Starter — $199/month: 1,000 minutes included; $0.20/min overage; up to 2 staff alert numbers.
  • Pro — $299/month: 2,000 minutes included; $0.20/min overage; up to 5 staff alert numbers; pickup, delivery & reservations.

  • Enterprise — $799/month: 10,000 minutes included; $0.15/min overage; unlimited staff alert numbers; Toast/Clover POS; monthly strategy call; 2-hour SLA.

Minutes are billed only for active call time within your configured hours. Each additional location beyond your first receives a 15% discount on the plan rate. A 7-day money-back guarantee applies to first-time subscribers.
